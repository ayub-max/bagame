FAQ

How do I buy a game?

Open any cover, press Add to cart, then checkout from the cart drawer. Purchased titles appear in Library.

Can I get a refund?

Yes — within 14 days if you played less than 2 hours.

Where is my progress saved?

Every session is stored on the History page and linked to your profile.

Do coupons stack with sales?

One coupon per order. Sale prices are already discounted; coupons apply on top when allowed.
